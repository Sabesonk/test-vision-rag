A page_no interval — the one filter shape

Comma-separated, at most 10 each. A filter, not a query
            term: a page that does not print the phrase cannot appear at any rank. Phrase
            matching, so safety output needs those words adjacent and in order — and
            each term also matches its whitespace variants, so SF 1.1A finds
            SF1.1A. A required phrase can never match a page whose text
            could not be read, so watch the usable-pages count below before reading an
            empty result as "the corpus does not have this".
